Developed with Robin Dheilly and Jessica Cauchard, NOX (ENtry, Occupancy, EXit) models human–robot territorial dynamics. It considers the expectations people hold about robots entering, occupying, and leaving spaces, and the tensions that arise when robot behavior conflicts with those expectations.

The paper received an **Honorable Mention (top 5%) at CHI 2026**.
