Attended the big [CHI26](https://chi2026.acm.org/) in Barcelona, where I presented my work (Honorable Mention) and contributed to two workshops: [Designing with Forest Stories to Explore What It Might Mean for Forest-Related Technologies to “Get It Right”](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3772363.3778772) and [Everything Is a Robot (and Nothing Is)](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3772363.3778719). As always, one of the best parts was reconnecting with people from around the world and exchanging ideas with researchers at different stages of their careers.
