Basics

Name Robin Bretin
Label HRI Researcher | Social Robotics, Spatial Interaction, Human Behavior & Theory
Email robin.bretin.research@gmail.com
Url https://robinbretin.github.io/
Summary I study how robots become part of human environments, focusing on interpersonal distance, territorial dynamics, and the social meanings of human–robot interaction. My work combines cognitive psychology, human-centered design, and experiments in real and virtual environments. Based in France and open to new opportunities in the European Union.
